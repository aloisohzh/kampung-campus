## Why

Pek Kio residents have skills and interests that could benefit neighbors, but struggle to find participants, secure resources, organize safely, and sustain groups beyond the first gathering. Kampung Campus provides the coordination layer to turn interests into recurring community activities, with a credit-based incentive system that rewards verified contributions with tangible benefits (vouchers, activities, learning opportunities) to encourage sustained participation.

## What Changes

- Build a resident-led community platform for organizing and discovering local activities
- Implement Kampung Credits system with starter credits (50 per resident), earned credits through verified contributions, and permanent contribution records independent of spending
- Create a four-pathway rewards catalogue (Everyday vouchers, Community activities, Sport & culture, Further learning) with base conversion of 10 credits = S$1
- Enable resident progression from Participant → Helper → Co-host → Organizer → Mentor with role-based credit earnings
- Provide organizer tools with three grant tiers (Meet/Make/Grow: S$0/S$150/S$750)
- Build operator console for managing missions, reward inventory, commitments, and financial reconciliation
- Ensure concurrent transaction safety (no double-spend, no duplicate approvals, credit type restrictions)
- Deliver an 8-week Pek Kio pilot demonstrating complete journey: verified contribution → credit issuance → reward selection → voucher redemption → funding reconciliation

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `activity-discovery-management`: Browse, search, and manage activities with interest groups, sponsored missions, commitment deposits, waitlists, and full lifecycle from proposal to delivery
- `resident-progression-system`: Track resident journey (Discover → Commit → Contribute → Verify → Earn → Redeem → Return) with role progression and contribution verification workflow
- `kampung-credits-system`: Manage three credit types (starter, earned, contribution record) with earning rules (5-20 credits per verified action), caps (100/period), validation (no duplicates, highest base only), and sponsor missions
- `rewards-catalogue-redemption`: Four-pathway catalogue with conversion rates, sponsor boosts, partner inventory, voucher generation, and single-use validation
- `resident-wallet`: Display and manage starter, pending, available, and reserved balances with transaction history and pending claims tracking
- `contribution-review-console`: Evidence submission, reward rule matching, independent approval workflow (no self-approval), rejection reasons, and appeals process
- `organizer-tools`: Activity proposal builder (including voice-based), co-host recruitment, demand demonstration, grant requests with three tiers, and delivery evidence submission
- `operator-console`: Mission funding configuration, reward inventory management, outstanding commitments tracking, settlement records, and financial backing verification (S$3K earned rewards + S$1.5K grants + S$500 ops budget)
- `merchant-validation`: Unique voucher validation with single-use enforcement, redemption confirmation, and duplicate prevention
- `partner-integration-framework`: Discovery assistance for existing credits (ActiveSG, Culture Pass), sponsored payment pathways, future SkillsFuture third-party sponsorship, with clear labeling of simulated vs. live integrations
- `governance-safety`: Age/consent/supervision rules, self-targeting prevention, proportionate verification without exposing private info, activity safety planning, dispute resolution, and data retention policies
- `pilot-evaluation-metrics`: Track 12 key metrics including first contribution (14 days), repeat contribution (60 days), role progression, fulfillment reliability, post-redemption participation, cost per contributor, participation gaps, and disputes

## Impact

- New standalone community platform (no existing system to modify)
- Requires integration readiness with partner systems (ActiveSG, Culture Pass, SkillsFuture) with clear simulation boundaries for pilot
- Pilot funding allocation: S$5,000 total (S$3K earned rewards backing for max 20,000 credits, S$1.5K organizer grants, S$500 ops/contingency)
- Needs appointed operator with owners for programme delivery, finance, safeguarding, partner fulfilment, and technical operations before live pilot
- 8-week pilot timeline: 2 weeks setup/recruitment, 4 weeks phased testing, 2 weeks evaluation, plus post-pilot redemption support and 60-day retention observation
